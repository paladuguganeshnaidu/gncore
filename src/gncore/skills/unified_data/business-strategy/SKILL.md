## Focused procedures

### Market Entry Strategy (`strategy-market-entry`)

Assess market size and quality, customer needs, competitors, regulation, localization, channel access, and unit economics. Show source dates and assumptions; define a low-cost validation gate before irreversible investment.

### Portfolio Prioritization (`strategy-portfolio`)

Use a consistent scoring model with confidence ranges, dependencies, resource demand, and strategic fit. Challenge sunk-cost bias and show which work loses capacity under each proposed portfolio.

### Competitive Analysis (`strategy-competitive`)

Use dated primary evidence where available; distinguish fact, inference, and unknown. Compare target segments, offer, pricing, distribution, differentiation, and likely response without presenting speculation as competitor intent.

### Business Model Review (`strategy-business-model`)

Trace customer, value proposition, channels, cost structure, revenue, key resources, and dependencies. Check contribution margin and incentives across the model; identify assumptions to validate before scaling.

### Strategic Scenario Planning (`strategy-scenario`)

Select a small number of material uncertainties, construct coherent scenarios, and quantify directional consequences. Define leading signals, robust actions, contingent options, and triggers; do not treat scenarios as forecasts.

### Strategy to OKRs (`strategy-okrs`)

Write outcome-based objectives, baseline-backed key results, owners, review cadence, and dependencies. Check that metrics are controllable, non-duplicative, and resistant to gaming; distinguish targets from forecasts.

### Pricing Strategy (`strategy-pricing`)

Define customer segments, packaging, price metric, discounts, costs, elasticity evidence, and competitor context. Model conversion and retention trade-offs; propose controlled tests and guardrails before broad changes.

### Enterprise Risk Assessment (`strategy-risk`)

Define risk events and time horizon, assess inherent and residual exposure, identify controls and owners, and document uncertainty. Use consistent scoring and distinguish risk appetite from a claim that risk is eliminated.

### M&A Opportunity Screen (`strategy-ma`)

Assess fit, synergies, integration load, valuation assumptions, diligence gaps, regulatory exposure, and alternatives. Label preliminary analysis clearly and route legal, tax, accounting, and investment conclusions to qualified reviewers.

### Business Transformation Plan (`strategy-transformation`)

Set a baseline, target operating model, workstreams, decision rights, change impacts, risks, and benefit owners. Sequence pilots and releases; track realized outcomes separately from promised savings.
