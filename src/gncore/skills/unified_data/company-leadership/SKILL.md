## Focused procedures

### CEO Decision Brief (`company-ceo`)

Synthesize strategy, financial position, customer outcomes, people capacity, risks, and decision options. State the decision owner, recommendation, trade-offs, leading indicators, and what evidence could change the recommendation; do not invent company facts.

### CFO Review (`company-cfo`)

Reconcile reported figures to their source and period, separate actuals from forecasts, explain cash and margin drivers, and show scenario sensitivities. Flag accounting assumptions and decisions that need a qualified controller or auditor.

### COO Operating Review (`company-coo`)

Map the end-to-end operating flow, service levels, capacity, constraints, owners, and failure costs. Prioritize a small set of measurable interventions and verify each action does not move the bottleneck or risk downstream service.

### CTO Technology Review (`company-cto`)

Connect technical choices to customer value, reliability, security, delivery capacity, and lifecycle cost. Distinguish observed system evidence from estimates and present reversible options before recommending platform-wide change.

### Chief Product Officer Review (`company-cpo`)

Compare opportunities by user problem, evidence strength, strategic fit, expected impact, delivery cost, and opportunity cost. Identify what to stop or defer and define measurable product outcomes rather than output targets.

### Chief Marketing Officer Review (`company-cmo`)

Connect audience, positioning, channel economics, pipeline attribution, brand measures, and budget. Separate incremental impact from correlation, expose attribution uncertainty, and recommend a testable allocation decision.

### Chief People Officer Review (`company-chro`)

Use aggregated workforce evidence, role capacity, retention, hiring, engagement, and employee relations context. Avoid unsupported causal claims or unnecessary personal data; identify fair, lawful, and measurable interventions.

### Chief Information Security Officer Review (`company-ciso`)

Describe assets, threat scenarios, business impact, control evidence, residual risk, and accountable owners in plain language. Distinguish verified incidents from hypotheses and define containment or investment gates without exposing secrets.

### Board Materials Review (`company-board`)

Separate items for decision, discussion, and information. Reconcile headline metrics, surface material risks and conflicts, state management's recommendation, and preserve confidential or legally privileged material appropriately.

### Executive Team Alignment (`company-executive-team`)

Identify the decision, competing incentives, dependencies, and unresolved assumptions. Record one accountable owner, contributing functions, decision deadline, success measures, and escalation trigger for each commitment.
