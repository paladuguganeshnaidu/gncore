## Focused procedures

### Customer Onboarding Plan (`customer-onboarding`)

Confirm desired outcomes, prerequisites, stakeholders, technical dependencies, timeline, and customer capacity. Define evidence of activation and escalate risks early without promising unapproved delivery dates.

### Support Case Triage (`customer-support-triage`)

Capture affected users, symptoms, start time, scope, workaround, environment, and sensitive-data considerations. Assign severity from documented policy, request minimal diagnostics, and avoid guessing root cause.

### Customer Support Reply (`customer-reply`)

Acknowledge the reported impact, state verified findings, give one concrete next step and owner, and set a truthful update time. Do not blame the customer or invent a fix timeline.

### Customer Escalation Brief (`customer-escalation`)

Summarize customer context, verified impact, actions taken, outstanding risks, communication commitments, and requested decision. Keep internal speculation out of customer-facing text.

### Customer Health Review (`customer-health`)

Define signal sources and freshness, distinguish product usage from realized value, include stakeholder coverage and open risks, and expose missing data. Treat health scores as triage aids, not objective truth.

### Customer Renewal Risk (`customer-renewal`)

Verify renewal date and terms, map outcomes achieved versus expected, open issues, decision makers, and recovery actions. Avoid pressure tactics and do not imply contractual rights without review.

### Support Knowledge Article (`customer-knowledge`)

Reproduce the supported scenario, state affected versions and prerequisites, order steps safely, include expected results and recovery, and link escalation criteria. Have another person follow the instructions before publishing.

### Voice of Customer Synthesis (`customer-voice`)

Preserve source, segment, date, and context; separate direct statements from interpretation; note sample bias and conflicting evidence. Recommend owners and validation, not a false consensus.

### Service Recovery Plan (`customer-service-recovery`)

Confirm impact and affected commitments, coordinate approved remedy, communicate with empathy and accuracy, and track completion. Do not admit legal liability or offer compensation outside authority.

### Customer Business Review (`customer-success-qbr`)

Use agreed success criteria and validated data, show progress and limits, include customer voice and unresolved issues, and align a small set of next actions with owners and dates.
