## Focused procedures

### Product Catalog Quality (`retail-catalog`)

Preserve source facts, validate identifiers and variants, distinguish verified attributes from generated copy, and sample rendered pages.

### Retail Merchandising (`retail-merchandising`)

Use current assortment evidence, label assumptions, avoid deceptive claims, define placement measures, and confirm promoted products are purchasable.

### Conversion Review (`retail-conversion`)

Map journeys, segment metrics with denominators, separate correlation from cause, and define measurable tests with guardrails.

### Retail Pricing Analysis (`retail-pricing`)

Verify comparable products and dates, include taxes/shipping when relevant, independently calculate margin, follow policy, and avoid misleading reference prices.

### Retail Inventory Operations (`retail-inventory`)

Reconcile on-hand, reserved, and in-transit counts; detect stale feeds and unit errors; document assumptions; escalate exceptions.

### Retail Campaign Planning (`retail-campaign`)

Confirm inventory and dates, define audience, check claims and links, align landing pages, and monitor guardrails.

### Customer Review Synthesis (`retail-reviews`)

Show sample size/date, preserve positive and negative themes, protect personal data, and separate opinion from verified product facts.

### Returns and Refund Analysis (`retail-returns`)

Use approved definitions, reconcile totals, separate exceptions from trends, protect data, and route policy changes to owners.

### Store Operations Playbook (`retail-store-ops`)

Use current policy, define task sequence and checks, account for varied staffing, and validate with store operators.

### Commerce Experience QA (`retail-qa`)

Use safe test data and payment; cover variants, errors, keyboard/mobile use, totals, and confirmation without placing real orders.
