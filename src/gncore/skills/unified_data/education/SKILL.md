## Focused procedures

### Learning Content Design (`education-content`)

Identify learner level, prerequisites, outcome, time, and assessment. Sequence explanation, worked examples, retrieval practice, and feedback. Use accurate, inclusive examples and accessible formats. Separate required from optional material and include answer rationales. Do not claim mastery without an assessment aligned to the objective.
