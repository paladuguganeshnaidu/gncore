## Focused procedures

### Venture Idea Screen (`venture-idea`)

Name the target customer, painful job, current workaround, frequency, willingness-to-pay evidence, and founder advantage. Rank assumptions by uncertainty and cost to test; do not confuse enthusiasm with demand.

### Customer Interview Guide (`venture-customer-interview`)

Ask about recent real behavior, consequences, current spend, alternatives, and decision roles. Avoid pitching before discovery and record exact evidence with consent; do not fabricate demand signals.

### Minimum Viable Product Scope (`venture-mvp`)

Specify hypothesis, target cohort, minimum capability, excluded features, success/failure threshold, instrumentation, and safe stop condition. Prefer a reversible pilot over building a full platform prematurely.

### Venture Business Model (`venture-business-model`)

Identify payer versus user, acquisition path, fulfillment, recurring costs, margins, retention, and dependencies. Mark each assumption with evidence and a test; show unit economics scenarios.

### Business Plan Draft (`venture-business-plan`)

Use source-linked market evidence, customer proof, product scope, channels, competition, operations, financial assumptions, risks, milestones, and funding use. Label projections as scenarios rather than guarantees.

### Fundraising Preparation (`venture-fundraise`)

Connect problem, solution, market evidence, traction definitions, economics, team, use of funds, and milestones. Reconcile every metric to a source, label forward-looking statements, and protect confidential information.

### Founder Runway Scenarios (`venture-runway`)

Reconcile cash and obligations, model hiring and revenue timing, identify minimum-cash triggers, and compare cost or growth options. State assumptions clearly and do not present this as investment advice.

### Early Go-to-Market Test (`venture-go-to-market`)

Select a narrow segment, offer, channel, budget cap, conversion definition, attribution plan, and decision threshold. Check consent and claim accuracy; avoid scaling on vanity metrics.

### Founder Alignment Checklist (`venture-founder-agreement`)

Record expectations, contributions, responsibilities, decision rights, departure scenarios, and unresolved assumptions. Do not draft definitive legal or tax terms; route agreements to qualified counsel.

### Pivot or Persevere Review (`venture-pivot-review`)

Compare original hypothesis and thresholds with customer behavior, retention, economics, and execution constraints. Separate external shocks from product failure and list next evidence needed for a bounded decision.
