## Focused procedures

### Positioning and Messaging (`marketing-positioning`)

Define audience, problem, alternative, differentiated value, proof, and category language. Use customer evidence and test comprehension; do not make claims the product cannot substantiate.

### Campaign Plan (`marketing-campaign`)

Set one business objective, target segment, message, channel sequence, spend, tracking plan, owner, and stop/scale rule. Check consent, brand, accessibility, and legal review needs before launch.

### Marketing Content Brief (`marketing-content`)

Specify reader intent, key takeaway, supporting evidence, CTA, channel, length, source material, review owner, and metric. Mark claims requiring verification and avoid filler or unsupported promises.

### Search Content Audit (`marketing-seo`)

Inspect target query intent, page accuracy, internal links, metadata, indexability, and performance evidence. Recommend updates that improve reader value; never promise rankings or stuff keywords.

### Lifecycle Messaging (`marketing-lifecycle`)

Map user state, trigger, helpful message, timing, frequency cap, opt-out, and success metric. Respect consent and user control; test holdouts where feasible and monitor complaints and unintended pressure.

### Marketing Attribution Review (`marketing-attribution`)

Reconcile event and spend sources, attribution window, identity coverage, channel overlap, and missing data. Separate attributed conversions from incremental lift and state sensitivity to the model.

### Brand Consistency Review (`marketing-brand`)

Compare assets to approved positioning, voice, visual system, legal claims, and accessibility needs. Identify inconsistencies with specific evidence and prioritize fixes by audience impact.

### Social Media Plan (`marketing-social`)

Set audience, purpose, cadence, format, source, moderation owner, response time, and escalation path. Verify claims and permissions; do not automate sensitive replies or fabricate engagement.

### Marketing Budget Allocation (`marketing-budget`)

Compare channel-level spend, incremental outcomes, saturation, lag, and confidence. Preserve test budget for unknown channels and model downside; distinguish average historical return from future marginal return.

### Go-to-Market Launch (`marketing-launch`)

Define segments, value proposition, packaging, channel, launch timing, enablement, proof, service readiness, and feedback loop. Gate external claims on verified product capability and owner approval.
