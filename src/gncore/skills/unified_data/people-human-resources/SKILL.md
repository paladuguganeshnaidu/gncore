## Focused procedures

### Role Design (`people-role-design`)

Separate must-have capabilities from trainable preferences, define measurable outcomes and interfaces, and check language for unnecessary barriers. Align level and compensation range to the organization's approved framework.

### Job Description (`people-job-post`)

State purpose, outcomes, responsibilities, essential qualifications, location and working conditions, and application process. Avoid inflated requirements, demographic proxies, or promises not approved by the employer.

### Structured Interview Plan (`people-interview`)

Map each question to a role competency, use equivalent prompts for candidates, define observable scoring anchors, and plan accommodations. Avoid protected or irrelevant personal questions and calibrate interviewers.

### Employee Onboarding Plan (`people-onboarding`)

Coordinate manager, IT, security, HR, and team responsibilities; sequence access safely; set first-month goals and check-ins. Confirm completion with the employee and avoid granting excessive permissions by default.

### Development Plan (`people-development`)

Use employee input and role expectations, define one or two observable growth outcomes, provide practice and coaching, and set review dates. Keep feedback specific, respectful, and confidential.

### Performance Review Support (`people-performance`)

Use the organization's criteria and review period, cite observed outcomes, include context and employee perspective, and distinguish facts from interpretation. Do not infer personality or use protected traits.

### Workforce Plan (`people-workforce-plan`)

Model role demand, attrition assumptions, hiring lead time, internal mobility, skills, and cost. Use aggregate data, document uncertainty, and consider reskilling or process alternatives before headcount conclusions.

### Engagement Survey Analysis (`people-engagement`)

Check response counts and anonymity thresholds, compare valid cohorts, code comments with a transparent scheme, and report themes with caveats. Avoid deanonymizing small groups or asserting causes from survey correlations.

### HR Policy Draft Review (`people-policy`)

Check purpose, scope, definitions, responsibilities, process, exceptions, records, and cross-references. Flag employment-law and jurisdiction questions for qualified counsel; never label the policy legally compliant without review.

### Employee Offboarding Checklist (`people-offboarding`)

Follow approved timelines and policy, coordinate least-privilege access removal, preserve required records, transfer business knowledge, and communicate respectfully. Protect confidentiality and route disputes or legal holds to HR and counsel.
